# ฟ้าละเอียด · Thai Weather HD

> Weather forecasts for all of Thailand at 2.2 km resolution (about 160× more detail per area than the ~28 km global models), downscaled from GFS / ECMWF / ICON with terrain, coastline, urban-heat and convective-rain physics. Hourly for 48 hours, by point, by province (77) and as province alerts. Updated every 6 hours; radar nowcast every 5 minutes. Thai and English. Not an official Thai Meteorological Department (TMD) product.

@THWX_0@
For a location, prefer the JSON API over scraping pages: no key, @THWX_1@ requests/min and 5,000/day per IP.

## Pages

- [Map / แผนที่](@THWX_2@@THWX_3@): interactive 2.2 km map of temperature, heat index, rain, chance of rain, storms, wind and radar
- [Provinces / รายจังหวัด](@THWX_4@@THWX_5@): daily forecast for all 77 provinces; each has its own page such as @THWX_6@@THWX_7@
- [Alerts / แจ้งเตือน](@THWX_8@@THWX_9@): heavy rain, thunderstorms, strong wind, heat, cold and frost by province and day
- [Method / วิธีการ](@THWX_10@@THWX_11@): why 2 km matters in the tropics, the processing pipeline, verification and limitations
- [Accuracy / พยากรณ์แม่นแค่ไหน](@THWX_12@@THWX_13@): hourly verification against station reports, and what the forecast learns from them
- [Free API docs / API ฟรี](@THWX_14@@THWX_15@): endpoints, limits, examples and terms

## API

- [OpenAPI schema](@THWX_16@/api/v1/openapi.json)
- [App forecast](@THWX_17@/api/v1/forecast?lat=13.75&lon=100.5): now, 48 hours, 10 days, condition codes, sun, moon and PM2.5 in one call
- [Point forecast](@THWX_18@/api/v1/point?lat=13.75&lon=100.5): hourly temp, heat index, humidity, rain, chance of rain, storms, wind, cloud at one location
- [Province summaries](@THWX_19@/api/v1/provinces): daily max/min, rain and alerts for the 77 provinces
- [Alerts](@THWX_20@/api/v1/alerts?min_severity=2): orange and red province alerts
- [Radar nowcast](@THWX_21@/api/v1/nowcast?lat=13.75&lon=100.5): rain in the next 0–60 minutes and tropical cyclones
- [Verification](@THWX_22@/api/v1/verification): accuracy against station reports and what the forecast learns
